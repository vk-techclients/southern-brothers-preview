The SBP Advantage

Choosing the right contractor is the most important decision on your project.

With Southern Brothers Properties, you're not just hiring a contractor — you're partnering with engineers who've performed under the most demanding standards in the world. Here is exactly what that means for your project.

Our Commitment

"Where the largest firms offer scale, SBP offers precision. We are not the oldest firm in Houston — but we are the most technically rigorous. That is our edge, and it is yours."

— Brian D. Washington & Russell Kelly II, Co-Founders

01

Houston Is Our Home — And Only Home

SBP operates exclusively in the Greater Houston metro area. No split attention across markets. No remote project management. We know this city's permitting landscape, its subcontractor network, and its building environment because this is the only place we work.

02

Engineers, Not Just Contractors

Our founders aren't contractors who picked up engineering terminology. They're degreed Mechanical Engineers from Southern University A&M with careers spanning NASA, Oil & Gas, and Medical Devices. That distinction means every SBP project starts with a technical blueprint and a zero-tolerance standard for guesswork.

03

3D Visualization Before Ground Breaks

Russell's proprietary computer modeling capability gives every client a photorealistic digital render of their completed project before construction begins. Fewer surprises. Fewer change orders. Faster approvals. A capability most large GCs still don't offer.

04

One Team. Total Accountability.

SBP manages every phase: pre-construction consulting, planning, permitting, construction execution, and final delivery. You have one point of contact for the entire lifecycle. No handoffs. No gaps. Total accountability from day one.

05

Commercial-First. Always.

95% of our portfolio is commercial. We haven't built our processes around residential and adapted them for commercial — we've built our entire operation for the complexity, compliance requirements, and institutional standards of commercial construction from the ground up.

06

In-House Real Estate Intelligence

Brian's active Texas real estate license creates a layer most general contractors simply cannot offer: construction decisions informed by real market value, acquisition strategy, and ROI analysis. Developer clients get a construction partner who thinks like an investor.

07

Pre-Construction Is a Science, Not a Formality

We don't rush to mobilize. Every SBP project begins with a rigorous pre-construction phase — detailed budgeting, phasing plans, subcontractor selection, and risk identification. The time we invest upfront is the time you save midway through.
