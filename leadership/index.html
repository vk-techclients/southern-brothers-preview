Leadership

Two engineers. One vision. Infinite precision.

Brian D. Washington

Co-Founder & Director of Operations

Brian brings 17+ years of design engineering experience to SBP's day-to-day operations. As Director of Operations, he is the operational backbone of every active build — coordinating project managers, crew leads, and subcontractors with the same precision he applied throughout his engineering career. Brian holds a Mechanical Engineering degree from Southern University A&M, is a licensed Texas real estate agent, and has built an extensive trade network across the Houston metro that gives SBP's clients a distinct procurement advantage. His dual expertise in construction and real estate means every project decision is informed by both build quality and long-term asset value.

Russell Kelly II

Co-Founder & Chief Estimator

Russell's career spans high-stakes project engineering roles at NASA, inside the Oil & Gas sector, and within the Medical Device manufacturing industry — making him one of the most technically diverse construction estimators in the Houston market. He brings to every project the same cost-control rigor and systems thinking that those industries demand. Russell specializes in large-scale commercial cost estimation and is the force behind SBP's proprietary 3D computer rendering process — a capability that allows clients to see their completed project before a single nail is driven, dramatically reducing change orders and construction surprises. Russell holds a Mechanical Engineering degree from Southern University A&M.

Our Combined Pedigree

Southern University A&M Mechanical Engineering graduates. Combined backgrounds spanning NASA, Oil & Gas, Medical Devices, and Licensed Texas Real Estate. 25+ years of engineering precision, now applied exclusively to Houston commercial construction.
