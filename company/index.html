Our Leadership

Experienced engineers and operators committed to delivering every project with precision and integrity.

Russell Kelly II

Executive Director of Project Operations

With over 20 years of hands-on engineering experience, Russell serves as Co-Owner and Executive Director of Project Operations, driving execution of the company's most ambitious construction ventures. He takes complex projects from cradle to grave — orchestrating conceptual design, strategic planning, and through-to-completion delivery. By blending technical engineering precision with integrated leadership, Russell ensures every project is delivered with uncompromising quality, on time, and on budget.

Brian Washington

Executive Director of Operations

A former mechanical engineer with 17 years of engineering experience, Brian oversees all day-to-day activities — on and off the job site — focusing on equipping teams with the tools, materials, and support needed to complete every project safely and on schedule. He personally guides clients through project walkthroughs, clearly breaking down scope and timelines so there are never any surprises. For Brian, exceptional construction is built on a foundation of trust and transparency.

Anthony Johnson

Executive Director of Business Development

Anthony leverages his engineering background to bridge technical expertise and strategic growth, seeking out new construction opportunities that expand the company's footprint. A natural people person, he thrives on building the trust and alignment necessary to turn initial conversations into successful, long-term partnerships. Whether crafting competitive proposals or representing the firm at major construction conferences, his goal is always to showcase the company's premier capabilities.

Pamela Washington

Executive Director of Project Coordination

Pamela serves in a vital hybrid role that blends field management with day-to-day logistical operations. She handles the critical intangibles that keep projects moving — overseeing strategic initiatives, tracking key deliverables, and managing confidential operations to keep the company organized, agile, and aligned. By managing stakeholder communications and keeping a sharp eye on project timelines, Pamela ensures projects consistently meet their budget and schedule goals.
