Subcontractor & Trade Partnerships

We Advocate for Skilled Tradespeople

Southern Brothers Properties builds its projects through a network of elite Houston-area skilled tradespeople and specialty subcontractors. We don't believe in a revolving door of labor — we build long-term relationships with the best tradespeople in Houston so our clients consistently get the same high-caliber execution on every project.

If you are a skilled tradesperson or specialty subcontractor operating in the Greater Houston area and you take quality as seriously as we do, we want to hear from you. SBP is growing — and we grow with the people who help us build.
