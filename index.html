About Southern Brothers Construction

Precision Engineering Meets Construction Excellence

Southern Brothers Construction is a full-service general contracting firm specializing in commercial construction and development. Founded by two Mechanical Engineers from Southern University A&M, we bring a technical precision to construction that sets us apart from traditional contractors. From large commercial renovations to custom builds — no project too complex, no challenge too intimidating.

Headquartered in Greater Houston, we serve clients across Texas and beyond — bringing unmatched local knowledge, permitting expertise, and subcontractor relationships built over years of hands-on work.

Why Choose Us

Engineers Who Build. Not Builders Who Guess.

Most contractors manage your project. Southern Brothers Construction engineers it. Our founders bring over 45 combined years of Mechanical Engineering experience from NASA, Oil & Gas, Robotics, and Aviation — industries where failure is not an option. That culture of precision, accountability, and systematic thinking is embedded in every project from day one.

Before a project begins, we always establish a solid foundation of communication — helping to eliminate surprises, reducing change orders, and giving you the clarity to make confident decisions. That's not standard practice in construction. At Southern Brothers, it's the only way we work.
