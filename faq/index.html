About SBP

What is Southern Brothers Properties (SBP)?

Southern Brothers Properties (SBP), LLC is a Houston-based real estate construction company specializing in all phases of commercial and residential construction — including custom builds, commercial renovations, and general contracting. Founded in 2017 by two Mechanical Engineers who graduated from Southern University A&M College, SBP brings an engineering-first precision to every project.

Who are the founders of SBP?

SBP was founded by Brian Washington and Russell Kelly — two college friends with degrees in Mechanical Engineering. Together they bring over 25 years of combined mechanical engineering experience across industries including NASA, Oil & Gas, and Medical Devices. Their technical background directly informs how SBP plans and executes every project.

When was SBP established?

Southern Brothers Properties was established in 2017 in Houston, TX. The idea was born from a desire to bring professional engineering discipline and genuine quality craftsmanship to the construction industry — an industry the founders felt lacked technical rigor.

Where does SBP operate?

SBP operates exclusively within the Greater Houston metropolitan area. This includes neighborhoods such as Downtown, Midtown, The Heights, the Energy Corridor, Galleria, Sugar Land, Katy, Pearland, Clear Lake, and The Woodlands. This focused service area allows us to build deep permitting knowledge, subcontractor relationships, and submarket expertise.

Services & Projects

What services does SBP offer?

SBP offers a full range of construction and contracting services including: Commercial Builds & Renovations, Custom Home Builds, Residential Planning, Full Concept-to-Completion Project Delivery, General Contracting (kitchens, bathrooms, game rooms, outdoor spaces), and Construction Consulting.

Does SBP handle both commercial and residential projects?

Yes. While our primary focus is commercial construction — spanning healthcare, corporate, industrial, education, and retail sectors — we also work with residential investors and developers on buy-box acquisition strategies and custom home builds in the Houston area.

What markets does SBP serve for commercial construction?

SBP builds across Houston's most vital commercial sectors: Healthcare & Medical (clinics, surgical centers, medical offices), Corporate & Office (headquarters, tenant improvements, build-outs), Industrial & Warehouse (distribution centers, manufacturing), Education (K–12, higher ed, institutional), Retail & Hospitality (restaurants, hotels, entertainment venues), and Developer Residential.

Does SBP handle concept-to-completion projects?

Yes. SBP is fully capable of taking a project from the initial concept phase all the way through final completion. Our engineering background means we can consult on design feasibility, manage permitting, coordinate subcontractors, and oversee construction — acting as a true single-source partner for your build.

Does SBP do 3D modeling before construction?

Yes — and this is a major differentiator. Before a single nail is driven, SBP models your completed project in 3D. This eliminates guesswork, reduces costly change orders mid-construction, and gives clients full visual clarity on what the final product will look like. It's standard practice at SBP, and uncommon in the broader contracting industry.

Working With SBP

How do I get started with SBP?

The easiest way to get started is to submit a project inquiry through our Contact page. Tell us your name, contact information, and a brief description of your project. Our team reviews all inquiries and will follow up within 1 business day to schedule a consultation.

What information should I have ready before contacting SBP?

It helps to have a general sense of: (1) the type of project — commercial build, renovation, or residential; (2) the intended use of the space; (3) your approximate timeline; and (4) a rough budget range. The more context you can share upfront, the more productive our initial conversation will be.

How does SBP ensure quality on its projects?

Quality control is embedded in the SBP process from day one. Our engineering backgrounds mean we approach every project with a systematic, document-driven methodology — detailed scopes of work, structured subcontractor management, on-site supervision, and regular client check-ins. We don't guess. We engineer.

Does SBP work with subcontractors?

Yes. SBP operates as a general contractor, meaning we manage a trusted network of skilled subcontractors across trades — electrical, plumbing, HVAC, framing, finishes, and more. All subcontractors working on SBP projects are vetted and held to our quality and professionalism standards.

Is SBP licensed and insured?

Yes. Southern Brothers Properties is a licensed and insured general contractor operating in the State of Texas and the Greater Houston area. We comply with all applicable local and state regulations, permitting requirements, and industry standards on every project we take on.

Does SBP offer consulting services?

Yes. In addition to full project delivery, SBP offers construction consulting for clients who need expert guidance on planning, feasibility, or managing an existing build. If you're not sure where your project stands or what it will take to complete it right, a consulting engagement with SBP can give you the clarity you need.
